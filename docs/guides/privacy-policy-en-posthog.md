This Privacy Policy explains how the HabitFable habit tracking service (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects personal information in accordance with applicable laws including the Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Items Collected
The Operator collects the minimum personal information necessary to provide the Service.
  • Social login (Google, Kakao, Naver, etc.): identifiers, email (if provided), nickname or name, profile image URL, within the scope allowed by the platform
  • The Service does not collect or store separate member passwords; login is via social login only.
  • Optional email registration: address entered and verified by the user
  • Automatically generated data during use of the Service: device/OS information, access logs, usage records, push (FCM) tokens, and similar data
  • Product and usage analytics (PostHog): usage events such as app launch, onboarding completion, login, habit creation, habit completion, and notification permission results; login provider type; app/OS version; build number; service member identifier; approximate region derived from IP; device and connection-related information (within the scope of the PostHog SDK and its service policies). This analytics data is designed not to include detailed user-entered content such as habit names, inquiry text, or email addresses. Screen recording (session replay) is not collected.
  • Banner ads (free use): advertising identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID) and related ad metrics may be collected per the ad platform policy.
  • Google Play in-app purchases (ad-free subscription): payment and subscription state are processed by Google; the Operator may receive the minimum information needed to provide the subscription (e.g. order/subscription status).
  • Habit and record data entered by the user
  • Inquiries: content and contact details you provide

2. Purposes of Use
  • Identification, membership, withdrawal, and maintaining social account linkage
  • Service provision, notifications, statistics, and quality improvement
  • Collection of usage behavior statistics through analytics tools such as PostHog, service and feature improvement, and understanding usage patterns
  • Banner ads and related measurement when using the free Service (where applicable)
  • Providing and distinguishing ad-free subscriptions
  • Optional email for account-related notices and recovery (if registered)
  • Fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance

3. Retention
The Operator deletes personal information without undue delay after purposes are fulfilled, except where retention is required by law.
  • Upon account withdrawal (deactivation): service use is restricted immediately; personal information and habit/service usage records linked to the account may be retained for up to one year (365 days) from deactivation and then deleted, except where law requires longer retention.
  • PostHog usage analytics: retained until account withdrawal (deactivation) or the processing purpose is fulfilled. Upon withdrawal, in-app analytics identification is cleared and the Operator requests deletion or de-identification of user-identifying information stored in PostHog within the scope of this Policy and PostHog policies. Aggregated statistical data may be retained in a form that does not identify individuals.

4. Third-Party Disclosure
The Operator does not disclose personal information beyond what is stated here, except with consent or as required by law.

5. Processing Entrustment
The Operator may entrust certain tasks to third parties as required by law, including disclosure of processors and scope.
  • Example: SMTP for transactional email.
  • Ads and payments: ad networks and Google Play may process data under their policies; where this constitutes entrustment, the Operator will disclose as required by law.
  • PostHog, Inc. (United States): entrusted tasks include collection, storage, and analysis of in-app usage events. Data may be processed and stored in the United States or other PostHog cloud regions (us.i.posthog.com). Retention follows Section 3 and until the entrusted purpose is fulfilled.
  ※ Due to use of PostHog, product and usage analytics information described in Section 1 may be processed and stored outside your country, including in the United States.

6. User Rights
Users may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing; the Operator will respond without undue delay. Upon withdrawal, the account is deactivated and related information is destroyed after the retention period in Section 3. Inquiries or deletion requests regarding product and usage analytics (PostHog) may be sent to khyun9512@gmail.com.

7. Destruction
Information whose retention period has expired or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed in an irrecoverable manner.

8. Security Measures
The Operator implements administrative, technical, and physical safeguards against loss, theft, leakage, alteration, or damage.

10. Changes to This Policy
Material changes will be notified at least 7 days in advance (30 days if unfavorable to users).
